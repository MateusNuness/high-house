Issue No. 07 — Structural Systems

The Load-Bearing Wall

Architecture is the will of an epoch translated into space.

— Ludwig Mies van der Rohe

The grid is not a cage; it is a load-bearing structure. Every column carries weight, every gutter distributes stress, and every module absorbs the vertical rhythm of the page.

Here, the primary subject is anchored hard to the left — eight columns of dense editorial mass. To the right, four columns of deliberate emptiness. The void is not absence; it is counterweight.

One rupture breaks the discipline: the title bleeds past the right margin by forty percent of its own width. It is the single crack in the wall — the point where the structure admits light.
